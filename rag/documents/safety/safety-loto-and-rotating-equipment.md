# Safety Instruction — Lockout/Tagout and Rotating Equipment

## Scope

Applies to all physical intervention on pumps, compressors, and motors covered by this document set: Boiler
Feed Pumps 101/102, Compressors C-201/C-202, Motors M-301/M-302.

## Before Any Physical Intervention

No physical inspection, adjustment, or repair on rotating equipment may begin until lockout/tagout (LOTO) is
complete, regardless of how minor the task appears. This applies even to tasks initiated from a troubleshooting
manual's "quick check" steps — a visual inspection near a coupling or shaft is not exempt.

## Lockout/Tagout Steps

1. Notify affected operations personnel and confirm the asset can be safely taken out of service.
2. Stop the equipment through normal shutdown procedure (see the relevant Operating Procedure document) —
   never lock out a running machine by de-energizing it abruptly unless responding to an immediate hazard.
3. Isolate all energy sources: electrical supply at the breaker, not just the local disconnect; process
   isolation valves on suction and discharge; any stored energy (e.g. residual pressure, spring-loaded
   components).
4. Apply personal lock and tag at each isolation point. Multiple workers on the same job each apply their own
   lock — a single lock does not cover a multi-person crew.
5. Verify zero energy state: attempt a start from the local control station (it should not start), and confirm
   zero pressure/flow at isolation points before opening any casing.
6. Only after verification is work authorized to begin.

## Rotating Equipment Hazards

- **Coupling and shaft contact**: guards must remain in place except during the specific inspection step that
  requires their removal, and must be reinstalled before re-energizing.
- **Stored rotational energy**: even after a motor is de-energized, a coupled shaft can continue rotating
  briefly; confirm full stop before any contact.
- **Pressurized fluid**: boiler feed pumps and compressors both handle pressurized process fluid; confirm
  depressurization at a local vent or gauge, not just at the control system display, before breaking any
  flange or fitting.
- **Electrical arc flash**: motor terminal box work requires appropriate arc-flash rated PPE per the site's
  electrical safety program, in addition to LOTO.

## Required PPE

Safety glasses and hearing protection at minimum in all unit areas; add hard hat, steel-toe boots, and
arc-flash rated PPE for any electrical panel or motor terminal work. Compressor areas may require additional
hearing protection due to sustained noise levels during normal operation.
